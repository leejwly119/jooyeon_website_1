Between Seats

Participatory Installation / Sculpture · 2026

  • Materials: Ceramic, metal, mechanical components, mixed media

Between Seats is a participatory installation in which the stability of a central structure depends on the presence and decisions of multiple participants.

A group of seats surrounds a ceramic form held within a metal system. As participants sit, their combined weight activates the mechanism and redistributes force toward the center. The work becomes temporarily stabilized through collective participation rather than through a fixed or autonomous support.

The installation is concerned with the moment when someone wants to leave. An individual decision is no longer entirely individual: withdrawing weight changes the conditions for everyone else and requires adjustment from the remaining participants. Participation therefore involves both agency and obligation.

The work developed through my research into healthcare and other shared infrastructures, where people rarely operate independently even when responsibility appears individualized. Patients, clinicians, families, institutions, and material systems continuously compensate for one another.

Rather than presenting cooperation as automatically harmonious, Between Seats treats interdependence as a negotiation. Stability exists only while participants continue to respond to changes in the system and to one another.
