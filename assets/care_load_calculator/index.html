Introduction

Who this tool is for

For people who keep the atmosphere from breaking

This website is for people who often soften their language, stay quiet, mediate tension, or leave conversations feeling drained. It asks whether emotional stability is being maintained collectively, or mostly by one person.

What it does

The quiz tracks adjustment, self-silencing, mediation, and emotional fatigue. Your answers are saved locally and used to generate a care load result.

Why care load matters

Small interactions reveal larger systems

Polarization is often discussed through elections, news, or ideology, but it also appears in everyday behavior: who speaks carefully, who stays silent, who prevents conflict, and who absorbs the discomfort of a tense situation.

Understanding care load matters because larger systems often depend on invisible emotional labor. In families, classrooms, workplaces, and political conversations, stability is not neutral. It is often produced by people who adjust themselves so that the group can continue functioning.

This tool makes that hidden labor easier to notice. It does not solve polarization, but it helps users ask a practical question: am I participating in a shared exchange, or am I carrying the work of keeping the exchange possible?
